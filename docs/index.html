What’s new

    1. Download on your Android phone

      Open this page on your phone and tap Download the beta. If your browser asks whether to keep the file, tap Download or Keep.

    2. Open the file

      Tap the notification when the download finishes, or find SpeakNow-beta.apk in your Downloads.

    3. Allow the installation

      Android protects you from apps that don’t come from the Play Store, so it asks first. Tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, then go back.

    4. Install and start

      Tap Install, then Open. The app guides you through the first steps and asks to use the microphone, which it needs to hear you read.

    “Unknown app” or Play Protect warning?

    That message appears for every app installed outside the Play Store, and SpeakNow isn’t there yet. If it appears, tap More details, then Install anyway. Only install SpeakNow from this page.

    Getting a new version

    Come back to this page and install the new file over the old one. Your progress is kept.

    Best results

    Read in a quiet place, hold the phone about 20 cm from your mouth, and wait for “Listening…” before you start. In the app, the free advanced analysis download (Offline content) gives the most precise scores.

    Your voice stays on your phone

    • Your voice is analysed on your phone, then forgotten. Nothing is recorded or sent.
    • No account, no ads, no tracking.
    • Your progress lives only on your phone. Export it or delete it whenever you like.

    Questions

    Is it free?

    Yes. The beta is completely free, with no ads.

    Is there an iPhone version?

    Not yet. SpeakNow is for Android phones for now.

    Do I need internet?

    Only to download extra content, such as natural voices and more texts. After that, you can practise anywhere, even offline.

    What level of English do I need?

    If you can read simple English sentences, you can use SpeakNow. Texts go from beginner to advanced, and you can practise with your own texts too.

    Will my progress be lost when the Play Store version arrives?

    Before switching, use Settings → Export my data in the app. You can restore it in the new version with Settings → Restore from a file.

    The app didn’t understand me. What can I do?

    Wait for “Listening…” before you speak, read at your normal pace, and avoid noisy places or Bluetooth headsets. For the most accurate results, install the free advanced analysis in Offline content.
